Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026 | App Name: BudgetWise

Welcome to BudgetWise, a privacy-first personal finance application developed by Codrix.dev ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy outlines how BudgetWise handles your data.

100% Offline & Private: BudgetWise is built strictly as an offline-first application. All financial records, budgets, and settings remain on your physical device. We do not operate remote servers, collect analytics, or share data with third parties.

1. Data Collection & Usage

BudgetWise allows you to log financial data to manage personal budgets. All information entered remains stored locally on your device:

  • Transaction Data: Amounts, categories, timestamps, notes, and recurring frequency.
  • Budgets & Savings Goals: Category allocation limits, goal target amounts, deposit records, and target dates.
  • User Preferences: Display name, currency preference, and theme mode.

2. Local Encryption & Storage Security

Your financial records are encrypted at rest on disk using SQLCipher 256-bit AES encryption. Database encryption keys are dynamically generated on your device and stored securely using OS-level keystore primitives (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain via Flutter Secure Storage).

3. Biometrics & Hardware Security

BudgetWise offers optional app lock capabilities using your device's biometric authentication (Fingerprint, Face ID, or PIN). Biometric authentication is handled entirely by the Operating System via standard APIs (LocalAuthentication). BudgetWise never captures, stores, or transmits your raw biometric data.

4. Push Notifications

BudgetWise uses local notification features (`flutter_local_notifications`) to schedule budget threshold alerts and weekly spending reminders locally on your phone. No remote push servers or external messaging providers are involved.

5. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We believe your personal finances should be private. BudgetWise contains:

  • NO Third-Party Advertising SDKs
  • NO Analytics or Telemetry Tracking (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Google Analytics)
  • NO Cloud Database Synchronization or Remote Server Backends

6. User Data Control & Complete Erasure

You maintain total control over your data. You can export your full transaction log at any time as a CSV file via the Settings screen. If you wish to delete all stored data, you can tap "Clear All Local Data" inside Settings > Data Safety, which immediately and permanently purges all local SQLite databases and secure storage keys.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions, the revised policy will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
